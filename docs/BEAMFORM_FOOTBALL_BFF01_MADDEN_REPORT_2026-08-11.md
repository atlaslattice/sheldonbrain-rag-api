# BFF-01 — Beamform Football One

**Technology status:** HYPOTHESIS / PROTOTYPE CANDIDATE. The architecture is mature enough to specify a test program; the integrated rig is not experimentally validated.

## Primary objective
Maximize probability of extinguishing a defined incipient vegetation fire and preventing reignition, subject to safety constraints.

## Secondary metrics
Time to extinction; reignition probability; water/mist/gel consumption; acoustic/electrical energy; acoustic field at target; environmental exposure; non-target effects; operational range.

## Four-arm control
1. **Control:** ignition, no suppression.
2. **Mist:** mist only.
3. **Bass:** acoustic forcing only.
4. **BFF:** synchronized acoustics + mist.

Use matched vegetation fuel, controlled moisture ranges, matched ignition energy, randomized trial order and repeated trials.

## Measure at the fire
Do not infer treatment from amplifier wattage/source specification alone. Measure sound pressure, particle velocity where feasible, waveform/harmonic content, phase, distance, wind, temperature/humidity, mist flux/droplet distribution, target temperature, extinction time and reignition.

## Parameter sweep
Frequency is empirical, not sacred. Sweep candidate low-frequency ranges while varying acoustic output, waveform, harmonic content, duty cycle, array geometry, phase/delay, mist flux, droplet distribution, distance, fuel moisture and wind.

**144 dB-class capability is a hardware-envelope reference, not a magic biological number.** Extreme-SPL testing requires remote/unmanned operation, measured exclusion zones, hard shutdown criteria, wildlife/crew protection and coordination with aviation operations.

## Promotion ladder
1. Published candle/pool-fire literature calibration.
2. Vegetation ignition coupon.
3. Burn tray / larger solid-fuel geometry.
4. Outdoor controlled plot.
5. Ember-generated spot-fire interception.
6. Prescribed-burn environment.
7. Operational prototype evaluation.

Every stage earns promotion through predefined success and safety criteria.

## Falsifiable core
**H1:** Acoustic+mist changes extinction probability and/or extinction time relative to mist alone for defined incipient vegetation fires.

**H2:** A phased/cardioid professional-audio architecture generates useful target-side acoustic forcing while materially reducing rear-zone exposure relative to an equivalent non-directional configuration.

**H3:** Closed-loop optical/thermal targeting improves suppression performance under moving/wind-perturbed ignition conditions relative to fixed actuation.

**H4:** Useful effects persist with enough distance and environmental complexity to justify progression toward prescribed-burn trials.

If H1 fails reproducibly, downgrade the acoustic suppression branch. If H2 fails, redesign or reject directional touring-audio geometry. If scaling collapses with distance/wind, retain the architecture only as an ignition-scale local tool.

## Open science principle
American, Chinese, African, European and other laboratories should be able to test the same claims. Successful replications, null results and failures are all Receipts.
